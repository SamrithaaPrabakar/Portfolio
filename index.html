Projects

FinGuard AI

Explainable Agentic Finance Controller

A finance-ops agent that doesn't just flag reconciliation mismatches — it investigates them, finds the systemic root cause, scores the risk, and recommends an action, while a human stays the only one who can actually approve it.

Match→ Investigate→ Explain→ Find Patterns→ Assess Risk→ Recommend→ Human Approval→ Audit
  • Three deliberately separated layers: deterministic pandas-based reconciliation and risk scoring, a tool-calling AI agent grounded on 9 real tools, and a human-in-the-loop approval workflow — no code path lets the AI silently touch a financial record
  • RAG retrieval over a real policy knowledge base and historical case archive (Postgres + pgvector) grounds every investigation in evidence, not invention
  • 100% reconciliation accuracy across 8 exception categories on 1,212 transactions, with 31/31 tests passing and a full append-only audit trail
